# Methodology

## Claim types
- **data_point:** a factual figure that can be checked against a source
- **prediction:** a forward call with a horizon
- **framework:** a causal claim tested over time against the scorecard

## Scoring
Status is one of: pending, correct, partly correct, wrong, unverifiable. Each resolution gets a date and a sentence explaining the evidence. Predictions are scored at their horizon, not before.

## Sources
To be filled in per pillar: source name, URL, update frequency, free or paid, collection method (manual or automated).

## Integrity
No deletions, no backfilling. Data is committed when collected.
